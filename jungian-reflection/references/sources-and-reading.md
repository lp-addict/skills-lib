# Sources and reading

Choose a work that answers the specific question. “CW” means Jung's *Collected Works*. These are reading routes. Check the relevant passage before attributing specific words, page numbers or paragraph numbers.

| Reader's question | Where to begin |
|---|---|
| What did Jung mean by individuation and the relation between ego and unconscious? | C. G. Jung, *Two Essays on Analytical Psychology*, CW 7, especially *The Relations Between the Ego and the Unconscious*. |
| How does Jung distinguish shadow, anima/animus and Self? | C. G. Jung, *Aion*, CW 9, part II. For a broader treatment of archetypal imagery see *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious*, CW 9, part I. |
| How did Jung approach dreams or the encounter between conscious and unconscious positions? | C. G. Jung, *The Structure and Dynamics of the Psyche*, CW 8, including “General Aspects of Dream Psychology” and “The Transcendent Function.” |
| Where can a newcomer start? | *Man and His Symbols*. Check the chapter author before attributing an idea to Jung; this volume has multiple contributors. |
| How can I read a scene from *The Red Book*? | Start with the supplied passage from *The Red Book: Liber Novus*. Distinguish the scene's speaking voice, Jung's commentary and editorial notes. |
| What did Jung write to Bill Wilson? | Locate their correspondence from 1961. Distinguish Wilson's narrative, Jung's reply and later accounts of their influence. |

For a close reading, anchor the response in a specific word, image, contrast or shift of voice in the supplied passage. Separate what the text says from the interpretation proposed. If the user asks about an exact quote, inspect the cited edition, a user-provided excerpt, or an identifiable primary source. Without access, label a paraphrase and omit unverifiable locators. Do not treat membership in a bibliography as proof of a quotation. Label later authors' revisions as later views. For reading suggestions that must be current or freely available, verify the requested details when answering.
